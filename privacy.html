Privacy Policy for Paintsweeper

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Paintsweeper (the “Game”) uses and why, on Android and on iPhone/iPad (iOS). The short version: the Game has no account system and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only outside services are the app store you got the Game from (Google Play or the Apple App Store, for purchases), its game service (Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, for achievements and leaderboards), and the advertising service described below.

2. Data that stays on your device

The Game stores the following only on your device, in files inside the app’s own storage:

  • your progress (completed levels, stars, best times, the board you are currently playing),
  • your settings (palette, sound, input options, and similar),
  • Daily Challenge streaks and achievement progress,
  • which in-app purchases you own (for example theme bundles), your star and hint balances, and which palette, stickers, and background you use.

I do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

3. Achievements and leaderboards

Android: Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games, the Game uses it to unlock achievements and to submit your Daily Challenge time to a leaderboard. Google processes your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name, avatar) and the achievements and scores sent by the Game. Google’s privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage or delete your Play Games data in the Play Games settings of your Google account.

iOS: Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the Game uses it in the same way. Apple processes your Game Center profile (player ID, nickname, avatar) and the achievements and scores. Apple’s privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. You can manage Game Center in Settings > Game Center.

  • Leaderboard entries show your player name and avatar to other players, according to your profile and visibility settings.
  • I receive no personal data about you through these services.
  • The Game works without signing in. Then no achievements or scores are sent.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example removing ads, theme bundles, stars, or hints) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. I do not receive your payment details. I receive only the confirmation that a purchase was made, which the Game stores on your device. Refunds follow the rules of the store you bought from.

5. Advertising

The free version of the Game shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show ads, the ad provider may process your advertising ID (on Android) or the identifier for advertisers, IDFA (on iOS), other app-scoped identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ads you have seen and your interaction with them, and crash and performance information of the ad software. It may use them to show personalised ads, to limit how often you see an ad, to measure ads, and to prevent fraud. For this, the ad provider may store and read cookies or similar identifiers on your device and may use web beacons and your IP address.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and similar regions, ads are only personalised and the advertising ID is only used after your consent. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the Game’s settings (“Privacy choices”).
  • If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data.
  • On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before the IDFA can be used for tracking (Apple’s “Allow app to track” prompt). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not used. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The Game’s features and rewards do not depend on this choice.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).
  • Buying “No Ads” removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded video (for example for free hints or to continue after a mistake) is always optional.
  • How Google uses data when you use apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The ad provider’s privacy information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Analytics and crash reports

The Game contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own. If the Game crashes, Google Play or Apple may give me anonymous crash and performance reports, if you have allowed sharing them with app developers in your device settings.

7. Third parties and security

I share data only with the services named above (Google Play, Google Play Games Services, Apple App Store, Apple Game Center, Google AdMob), and only to provide the Game, purchases, and ads. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and by data protection law, and give your data at least the same level of protection as this policy describes. I do not sell data myself and do not share it with anyone else.

The Game keeps its data in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Its connections to Google’s services are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). I run no servers and keep no copies of your data.

8. Legal bases (for users in the EU/EEA, UK)

  • Playing the Game, saving your progress, and handling purchases: performance of the agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Advertising identifiers and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.
  • Play Games features: your choice to sign in, handled by Google.

9. Retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data (Android: Settings > Apps > Paintsweeper > Storage > Clear data; iOS: delete the app). This deletes it completely. I do not keep any other data about you.
  • Ad consent: change or withdraw it at any time in the Game under Options > Privacy choices, and on iOS also under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in your device settings.
  • Achievements and leaderboard entries: manage or delete them in the Play Games settings of your Google account, or in Game Center on iOS.
  • Google and the ad provider keep data according to their own policies (see the links above).
  • For any other request, write to m.zaouchi1997@gmail.com. I answer within one month.
