Privacy Policy for Paintsweeper

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Paintsweeper (the “Game”) uses and why. The short version: the Game has no account system and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only outside services are Google Play (purchases and Play Games) and the advertising service described below.

I am the controller of the data processing described here, as far as I decide how it is done. Google and the ad provider process data under their own responsibility for their own services.

2. Data that stays on your device

The Game stores the following only on your device, in files inside the app’s own storage:

  • your progress (completed levels, stars, best times, the board you are currently playing),
  • your settings (palette, sound, input options, and similar),
  • Daily Challenge streaks and achievement progress,
  • which in-app purchases you own (for example theme bundles), your star and hint balances, and which palette, stickers, and background you use.

I do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

3. Google Play Games Services (Android)

If you use Play Games sign-in, the Game uses Google Play Games Services to unlock achievements and to submit your Daily Challenge time to a leaderboard. For this, Google processes your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name, avatar) and the achievements and scores sent by the Game.

  • Leaderboard entries show your Play Games name and avatar to other players, according to your Play Games profile and visibility settings.
  • I receive no personal data about you from Google through this. Google’s Play Games policies and privacy policy apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can leave the leaderboard and manage or delete your Play Games data in the Play Games settings of your Google account.
  • The Game works without signing in. Then no achievements or scores are sent.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example removing ads, theme bundles, stars, or hints) are handled by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details. I receive only the confirmation that a purchase was made, which the Game stores on your device. Refunds follow the Google Play rules.

5. Advertising

The free version of the Game shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show ads, the ad provider may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with ads. It may use them to show personalised ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and similar regions, ads are only personalised and the advertising ID is only used after your consent. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the Game’s settings (“Privacy choices”).
  • You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • Buying “No Ads” removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded video (for example for free hints or to continue after a mistake) is always optional.
  • Google explains how it uses data from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The ad provider’s privacy information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Analytics and crash reports

The Game contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own. If the Game crashes, Google Play may collect anonymous crash information for me if you have allowed that on your device.

7. Legal bases (for users in the EU/EEA, UK)

  • Playing the Game, saving your progress, and handling purchases: performance of the agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Advertising identifiers and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.
  • Play Games features: your choice to sign in, handled by Google.

8. Retention

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. I do not keep any other data about you. Google and the ad provider keep data according to their own policies.

9. Your rights

Under data protection law you may have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right to withdraw consent. Because I hold no personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google (Play Games, your Google account) or to the ad provider. For anything else, write to m.zaouchi1997@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the Game’s online features, contact me and I will help.

11. International transfers

Google and the ad provider may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. They rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy framework.

12. Changes

If the Game’s features change (for example a new ad network or new online features), I will update this policy and the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or in the store listing.
